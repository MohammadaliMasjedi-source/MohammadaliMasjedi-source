# Mo Masjedi

I'm Mo — formally **Mohammad Ali Masjedi**. I develop software, study battery life, play classical guitar and teach. Music, engineering, learning and cultural work keep bringing me back to listening, experimenting and making things with other people.

I work in software development at SincoTec and am completing my master's studies in Informatics at TU Clausthal. My research explores early-cycle battery remaining useful life prediction with Python, PyTorch and sequence models. Earlier work includes software engineering, project management and solutions architecture at Hesabfa.

Neda Aryana and I co-founded **Shiraz Art House (SHAH / خانهٔ هنر شیراز)**; I served as CEO from 2014 to 2020. My teaching experience spans school computing, music history, guitar, music theory and individual tuition.
